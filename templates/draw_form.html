How the Qor3a Algorithm Works
  1. Categorization: Players are grouped by (gender, age group, weight category/type)
  2. Random Shuffling: Players within each category are randomly shuffled
  3. BYE Handling: For odd numbers, one random player gets a BYE to next round
  4. Match Creation: Sequential pairing creates first-round matches
  5. Bracket Generation: Complete tournament tree is generated
  6. Fair Play: Random shuffling ensures no systematic advantage
Age Groups:

  • Junior: 18-34 years
  • Senior: 35+ years
Weight Categories (Kyourgi):
  • Light: Up to 68kg
  • Middle: 68-80kg
  • Heavy: Over 80kg
